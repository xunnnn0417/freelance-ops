# Paid Acquisition Test Policy — 2026-10-06

Goal: get the first legitimate paid freelance revenue faster without turning platform spending into uncontrolled sunk cost.

## Global rule
Paid access is no longer an automatic reject. A paid feature may be used only after:
1. The profile/service page is already good enough to convert.
2. There is a specific high-fit opportunity or a clearly measurable lead mechanism.
3. The expected acquisition cost is small relative to conservative project value.
4. The platform does not require a long commitment when a small test exists.
5. No payment is made automatically without the user's explicit approval.

## Current platform policy

### PRO360
- Highest-priority paid test candidate.
- Prefer manual quotes where the displayed fee is known before sending.
- Official docs say manual quote cost is dynamic; customer read triggers the charge, and eligible unread quotes can be refunded after 48h.
- Also check whether the Data Entry service supports Promotion Service: estimates are free and the platform charges only when a customer proactively contacts the expert.
- Initial gate: target quote-acquisition cost <= 5–10% of conservative job value.
- Do not buy a large balance before seeing current per-case quote fees and any account bonus/credit.
- If the minimum purchase required is > NT$500, reassess before buying.

### Tasker
- Free proposals remain the default.
- TaskerGo is a controlled test candidate only when at least 2–3 fresh, high-fit, low-competition cases are available inside the 30-day window.
- Starter T Points pack: NT$600 / 600 points.
- Do not buy TaskerGo just to boost weak or stale cases.

### Upwork
- Project Catalog has been submitted for review, so keep using the free inbound route.
- If paying, buy Connects directly rather than Freelancer Plus unless Plus benefits are independently justified.
- Connects are US$0.15 each.
- Only buy enough for a small set of pre-screened jobs; no boosts/Availability Badge by default.
- Initial paid test should target recent jobs, low competition, clear scope, and budget >= US$100 where possible.

### Contra
- Keep Free for now.
- Pro is US$29/month or US$199/year.
- Do not buy Pro until the profile/portfolio is substantially complete and Free feed/public discovery has been tested.
- Reassess a one-month Pro test only if there are multiple clearly high-fit jobs hidden behind the Pro wall.

## Spending priority
1. PRO360 small per-case/manual quote test if current fee is reasonable.
2. TaskerGo starter pack only when fresh high-fit inventory appears.
3. Upwork direct Connects only for pre-screened jobs.
4. Contra Pro: hold.

## QA before any purchase
- Confirm exact amount and whether it is refundable/recurring.
- Confirm what feature is unlocked.
- Confirm at least one immediately actionable target.
- Calculate break-even against conservative project value.
- Get explicit user approval for the payment.
