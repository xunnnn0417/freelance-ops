# Freelance Workflow

## 1. Source
Search live jobs on reputable Taiwan and international platforms. Prioritize fresh listings, low/acceptable competition, credible payment history, low fixed tool cost, and tasks deliverable with research, spreadsheets, automation, browser work, or AI-assisted workflows.

## 2. Screen
For each candidate check:
1. Safety/red flags: platform-off payment pressure, advance payment requests, suspicious downloads, credential requests, unrealistic guarantees.
2. Economics: expected hours, effective pay, platform fees/Connects/tool costs, revision risk.
3. Deliverability: required data, tools, skills, deadlines, QA burden, legal/identity boundaries.
4. Client quality: spend/hire history, recent activity, clarity, scope stability.

## 3. Prepare
Create a case folder with:
- `brief.md`
- `execution-plan.md`
- sample/demo/template where useful
- QA checklist
- proposal/client messages

Evaluate open-source tools only when they materially improve accuracy, QA, automation, formatting, reliability, or presentation. Record license obligations before reuse.

## 4. Apply
Client-facing copy must be concise, natural, credible, specific, and truthful. Never claim credentials, client history, ratings, paid tools, or results that do not exist. Prefer a small relevant sample over generic promises.

## 5. Execute
After contract/client data is available:
- confirm scope against the case brief
- work in checkpoints
- normalize/validate outputs
- preserve source traceability where appropriate
- avoid guessing unverifiable fields

## 6. QA
Run factual, formatting, duplicate, completeness, and deliverable-openability checks. Review the final artifact from the client's perspective before delivery.

## 7. Deliver / Collect
Send a short delivery note summarizing what was completed, notable limitations, and file structure. Keep platform payment inside the platform unless the platform explicitly supports another safe flow.

## User-only boundaries
Stop for: first-time registration, login/2FA if credentials are not already available to an authorized tool, identity verification, legal/recording releases, formal contract acceptance, purchase of Connects or paid tools, or access to private client files not yet provided.

## Public-repo rule
Until this repository becomes private, only commit public/sanitized case information. Never commit credentials, private client files, personal emails/phones, unpublished datasets, contract-confidential material, or anything restricted by platform terms.
