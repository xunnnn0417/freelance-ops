## QA mini-protocol
1. Compare sample waveform/audio and text; mark uncertain segments rather than hallucinating words.
2. Check 1-1 timestamp alignment, overlapping speech, speaker ID continuity, punctuation and zh-TW orthography.
3. Produce discrepancy table: start/end timestamp, issue category, original snippet, corrected snippet if allowed, uncertainty note.
4. Apply platform confidentiality policy; never upload private audio to public repo or external models without explicit permission.
